What is Light Extinction (κ)?

Governs shortwave solar radiation absorption via the Beer-Lambert law: I(z) = I₀ · exp(-κz).

  • Low κ (≤ 0.3 m⁻¹): Oligotrophic / deep clear lakes (e.g., Lake Superior ≈ 0.2, Stechlin ≈ 0.3). Solar heat penetrates deeply, delaying surface warming and deepening the thermocline.
  • Moderate κ (0.4 – 0.9 m⁻¹): Mesotrophic lakes (e.g., Müggelsee ≈ 0.7, Geneva ≈ 0.4). Balanced heat trapping.
  • High κ (≥ 1.0 m⁻¹): Eutrophic / turbid waters (e.g., Heiligensee ≈ 1.2, Balaton ≈ 1.8). Heat is concentrated in top centimeters, creating warm shallow epilimnia.
  • Rule of thumb: Secchi depth Z_SD ≈ 1.7 / κ; Euphotic zone Z_eu ≈ 4.6 / κ.

About FLake & WebAssembly Compilation

FLake (Freshwater Lake model) is a thermodynamic two-layer lake model originally developed by Dmitrii Mironov and collaborators at the German Weather Service (Deutscher Wetterdienst, DWD) and the Leibniz Institute of Freshwater Ecology and Inland Fisheries (IGB Berlin). It is widely incorporated into numerical weather prediction (NWP) and climate modeling systems (e.g. COSMO, ICON, ECMWF IFS).

Concept of Self-Similarity

Rather than solving many vertical grid levels, FLake models the lake as an upper uniform mixed layer (epilimnion) and a stratified thermocline (metalimnion) whose shape is parameterized by a self-similar polynomial profile preserving heat and mass conservation.

Compiled with LFortran

This application runs the authentic Fortran 90 physics code of FLake compiled directly into WebAssembly using the LFortran 0.68.0 modern Fortran compiler and LLVM. The resulting binary is only 26.4 KB in size.

Zero Backend & Offline

Every single ODE integration timestep executes locally in your browser's WebAssembly runtime with zero network roundtrips, zero server compute, and instant physical feedback.

Weather API Real-World Reanalysis & Forecasts

Fetches real hourly atmospheric time series for any latitude and longitude on Earth via Open-Meteo ERA5, NASA POWER, or offline analytical climatology.

Data Source: Hourly ERA5 / DWD / ECMWF API Best For: Real-lake historical validation & short-term forecasting
Perpetual Year Cyclic Thermal Equilibrium

Iterates a 1-year 3-hourly atmospheric cycle repeatedly until the water column converges to exact thermal equilibrium (|ΔT| ≤ 0.05°C), classifying the natural limnological mixing regime (Dimictic, Monomictic, Polymictic).

Data Source: GDAS1 / ERA5 representative annual climatologies Best For: Steady-state regime classification, mean ice duration, seasonal thermocline stability
Curated Benchmark Lake Observational Records

Peer-reviewed observational meteorological datasets for iconic lakes (Müggelsee, Heiligensee, Stechlin, Geneva, Ladoga, Superior, etc.) with verified depth and clarity values.

Data Source: Published scientific calibration records Best For: Academic verification, reproducing published FLake benchmarks
